Fart Box — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Fart Box has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to.

The app does show ads, and the ad network does collect some information. That section is below and it is the only part of this policy where anything leaves your phone.

Microphone

Two features use the microphone, and only while you have switched one of them on:

  • Break the Silence waits for a quiet room and then ends it.
  • Farting Interference answers any noise louder than a level you set.

For both of these the app reads exactly one number from the microphone: how loud the room is right now. No audio is recorded, no file is created, and nothing is sent anywhere. The reading exists for a fraction of a second and is replaced by the next one.

The microphone is off unless one of those two features is switched on. Turning the feature off releases it immediately.

Recording your own sounds

The My Farts section of the Fart Box screen lets you record up to ten sounds of your own, five seconds each. These are real recordings and they are saved as files.

  • They are stored in the app's own private storage on your device, where no other app can read them.
  • They are never uploaded. The app has no server and no upload of any kind.
  • There is no way to send one to another person from inside the app. This is deliberate, not an oversight.
  • Holding a slot deletes that recording. Deleting the app deletes all of them.

You control what gets recorded, because nothing records until you tap an empty slot, and it stops on its own after five seconds.

Advertising

Fart Box shows a banner advertisement, served by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob collects information from your device — typically your Advertising ID, your IP address and coarse information about the device and the app. It uses these to select and measure ads, and it may share them with its own advertising partners.

We do not receive that information, and we have no way to connect it to you.

  • Google's handling of it is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation entirely, in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your phone.
  • In the UK, the EEA, Switzerland and anywhere else the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before it requests a single ad, using Google's own consent form. Declining does not switch the ads off; it makes them non-personalised. No ad is requested while consent is withheld.
  • In US states with privacy laws, such as California, ads may use this information unless you opt out.
  • Either way, Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app reopens that form so you can change your mind, including choosing not to have your data sold or shared. The entry appears wherever the law gives you that right.

What else the app keeps on your phone

In the app's own private storage, never transmitted:

  • How many sounds you have played, in total.
  • Which achievements you have unlocked.
  • Which sound packs you have unlocked.
  • Your settings, including the gap between sounds and whether features keep running in the background.

None of this is backed up off the device. Android's automatic backup is switched off for this app deliberately, so uninstalling really does delete everything, and nothing is copied to your Google Drive.

Sharing

When you share a sound or an achievement, the app hands a file or a line of text to your phone's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes. The app has no connection to any social network and learns nothing about what you did with it.

Children

Fart Box is a novelty sound app and its humour may appeal to children. It is not directed at children, and it is not listed for a child audience.

We collect nothing from anyone directly. The advertising described above is provided by Google and is subject to Google's own policies, including their treatment of users it identifies as children.

Your choices, in one place

  • Refuse the microphone permission, or withdraw it later in your phone's settings. Every other part of the app works without it.
  • Delete any recording by holding its slot.
  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes everything it has stored.
